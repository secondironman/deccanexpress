Our Story

From the Streets
of Hyderabad

Deccan Express was born from a simple longing — the smell of dum biryani wafting through the lanes of the old city, the thick sweetness of an Irani chai at dusk. We brought that feeling to Ireland, one pot at a time.

Every dish is made with spices sourced from Hyderabad, cooked low and slow with the same love your nani would recognise.

Who We Are

Carrying Hyderabad
to Ireland

Deccan Express is a home-grown food venture built on passion, tradition, and the belief that great biryani knows no borders.

We cook every pot with spices brought from the heart of the Deccan plateau — no shortcuts, no compromises. Just the real thing, made for you.

01
Authenticity First
Every recipe is rooted in the old-city traditions of Hyderabad. We cook the way it's always been done — slow, patient, and with care.
02
Made Fresh Daily
We operate on pre-orders so every plate is cooked fresh for you. No reheating, no compromise on quality.
03
Community Rooted
